Pro Labs & Cyber Ranges

The map above is exams. This map is ranges. Pro Labs and cyber ranges are large, multi-host simulated enterprise networks you attack end-to-end — getting a foothold, pivoting, escalating, and reaching domain/cloud dominance. They are not proctored certifications: instead of an exam, finishing one earns a certificate-style completion acknowledgement / badge. That makes them ideal for building and proving hands-on skill — preparing for OSCP / CRTO / CPTS, rehearsing real red-team and AD tradecraft, or showing a hiring manager you can clear a realistic environment. Difficulty follows HTB's own scale — Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced (left-to-right). HTB ships these in two sizes: full Pro Labs and shorter Mini Pro Labs. Much of the Mini Pro Labs catalogue (and the Wutai / Shinra Pro Labs) originated on VulnLab (by xct), which HTB acquired and folded in — those carry an ex-VulnLab note. A parallel enterprise-only tier mirrors all of this for teams — see the block at the bottom.

Enterprise-only — HTB Enterprise Platform (not sold to individuals)

The same content runs on HTB's business platform under different containers, provisioned per-organisation for team training, onboarding and benchmarking. You can't buy these as an individual — they require an HTB Enterprise contract. Dedicated Labs also carries exclusive content not on the public side (recently: AI supply-chain exploitation, malware analysis, identity-governance abuse, satellite challenges).

Prices are approximate and change frequently — verify on the provider site before purchasing; some volatile ones read "Check provider". For GIAC, the figure is the exam/certification attempt only (~$979); the associated SANS training course is separate and far higher (typically ~$8–9k). "Course req." means the cert effectively requires the vendor's own training/lab (you generally can't challenge the exam independently).

Levels follow a Foundational→Intermediate→Advanced→Expert progression (green→blue→orange→red on the left rail); ★ marks a handful of elite / flagship credentials within Expert. Use the Pathway selector to spotlight a suggested route (e.g. eJPT → CPTS → OSCP → OSEP).
